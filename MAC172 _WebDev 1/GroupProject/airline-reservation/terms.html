Legal

Conditions of carriage

Written to be read. If a clause below needs a lawyer to understand, that is our mistake and we would like to hear about it.

1. What a ticket is

A confirmed booking is a contract between you and Kestrel Air to carry you and your baggage between the airports shown, on the date shown. The record locator identifies that contract. Fares are quoted in US dollars and include all federal taxes and security fees.

2. Changes and cancellations

You may change to another Kestrel flight on the same route at no charge at any time before departure. If the new fare is lower, the difference is issued as a travel credit valid for twelve months. If it is higher, you pay the difference only.

A cancellation before departure is refunded as a travel credit. A cancellation within 24 hours of booking is refunded to the original card in full, as required by US Department of Transportation rules.

3. Baggage

Every fare includes one personal item and one full-size carry-on, up to 22 x 14 x 9 inches. Checked bags cost $35 for the first and $45 for the second, up to 50 pounds each. Kestrel Club members check the first bag free.

We are liable for checked baggage up to $4,700 per passenger on domestic flights. Fragile items, electronics, medication and documents should travel in the cabin.

4. Delays and cancellations by us

If we delay a departure by more than three hours for a reason within our control, we issue a $150 travel credit automatically to the email on the booking. You do not need to claim it.

If we cancel a flight, you may take the next available Kestrel flight at no charge or take a full refund to the original form of payment. We do not require you to choose the credit.

5. Seats

Main-cabin seat assignment is free. Extra-legroom and first-class seats carry the fee shown at the time of selection. We may move you to a different seat of the same class for safety or operational reasons; if we move you to a lower class, we refund the difference.

6. Conduct

We may refuse carriage to a passenger whose conduct endangers the aircraft, the crew or other passengers. This is the only circumstance in which a fare is forfeited.

7. Privacy

We collect the information needed to carry you: names, contact details, payment details and travel documents. We share it with the Transportation Security Administration where the law requires it, and with no one else. We do not sell passenger data.

8. This is coursework

Kestrel Air is a fictional carrier built as a student project for MAC172 Web Development I. No booking made here is real, no payment is processed, and no data leaves your browser. Everything you enter is kept in your own browser's local storage and disappears when you clear it.
