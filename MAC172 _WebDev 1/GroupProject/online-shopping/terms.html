Terms of Service

Last updated 19 October 2026.

These terms cover orders placed on this site. They are written plainly on purpose. If something here is unclear, ask us before you order.

1. Placing an order

An order is an offer to buy. We accept it when we send the shipping confirmation, not when you submit the form. If an item sells out between those two moments we will cancel that line and refund it in full.

2. Prices and tax

Prices are in US dollars and exclude sales tax. Orders shipped within New York State are charged the combined state and city rate shown at checkout.

3. Returns

Return anything unworn and tagged within 60 days for a full refund. We pay return postage on faulty goods; you pay it on a change of mind.

  • Start a return from your account, or by contacting us.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days.
  • Made-to-order and final-sale items cannot be returned.

4. Repairs

Knitwear and bags carry a two-year repair promise. Send the item back and we will mend it where a mend is possible, or replace it where it is not.

5. Your account

Keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what happens on your account. Tell us at once if you think someone else has used it.

6. Your information

We collect the name, address, email and phone number needed to fulfil your order, and nothing else. We do not sell personal information.

7. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of New York.
