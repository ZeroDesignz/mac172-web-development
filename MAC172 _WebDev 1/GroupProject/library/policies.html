Borrowing policies

Last updated 19 October 2026.

These rules apply to every card. They are written plainly on purpose. If something here is unclear, ask a librarian.

1. Getting a card

Cards are free to anyone who lives, works or studies in Queens. Bring photo identification and something showing your address to any branch. Cards for borrowers under 18 need a parent or guardian signature and carry no fines.

3. Renewals

Renew from My loans at any hour. A renewal runs 21 days from the day you renew, not from the old due date, so renewing early costs you nothing. An item cannot be renewed once somebody has placed a hold on it.

Borrowing is suspended once a card owes more than $15. Paying the balance restores it the same day.

5. Holds

  • Place a hold on anything currently out. There is no charge.
  • Holds are filled in the order they were placed.
  • We email you when a copy arrives, and hold it at the desk for seven days.
  • A hold not collected within seven days goes to the next person waiting.

6. Privacy of records

Under New York Civil Practice Law and Rules § 4509, library records identifying what a person borrowed are confidential. We do not disclose them except on a court order. Loan history is deleted once an item is returned and any fine is settled.

7. Code of conduct

Branches are shared space. We ask for quiet in the reading rooms, no food at the computers, and that children under eight stay with an adult.

Bridgeview Public Library is a fictional institution built as coursework for MAC172 Web Development I at LaGuardia Community College. No real cards are issued and no personal information is collected or transmitted.
