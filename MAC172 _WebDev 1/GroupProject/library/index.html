How borrowing works

Three weeks per loan

Every item goes out for 21 days, counted from the day you borrow it.

Two renewals

Renew twice from My loans, 21 days each time, as long as nobody is waiting.

25¢ a day late

Capped at $10 per item. Nothing is owed by borrowers under 18.

Holds on anything out

Place a hold and we will email you the day a copy is back on the shelf.
