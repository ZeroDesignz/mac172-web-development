Membership agreement

Last updated 19 October 2026.

This agreement covers every Meridian account. It is written plainly on purpose. If anything here is unclear, ask us before you open an account.

1. Membership

Meridian is a not-for-profit cooperative. Opening an account makes you an owner, not a customer: you hold one vote at the annual meeting regardless of your balance. Membership is open to anyone who lives, works or studies in Queens or Brooklyn, and to their immediate family.

2. Your deposits are insured

Accounts are federally insured by the National Credit Union Administration to at least $250,000 per depositor, per ownership category. That insurance is backed by the full faith and credit of the United States Government.

4. Rates

Deposit rates are variable and may change at any time at the discretion of the board. Loan rates quoted on this site are the rates available to members with strong credit; your rate is set when the application is approved.

5. Transfers and payments

  • Transfers between your own Meridian accounts clear immediately.
  • ACH transfers to another institution take one to three business days.
  • Scheduled bill payments can be cancelled until 5pm the business day before.
  • We may decline a payment that would overdraw an account.

6. Unauthorised transactions

Tell us at once if you believe your card or password has been used without your permission. Under Regulation E, if you report a lost or stolen card within two business days your liability is capped at $50. After that it can rise to $500, and after sixty days from the statement date it may be unlimited.

7. Privacy and security

We collect the information needed to operate your accounts and meet our obligations under the Bank Secrecy Act, and nothing beyond it. We do not sell member information. We will never ask for your full password, your PIN, or a one-time code by phone, email or text. If someone does, it is not us.

8. Closing an account

You may close any account at any time with no fee. Any balance is returned by cheque or transfer within five business days.

9. Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York and the Federal Credit Union Act.
