Rental terms

Last updated 19 October 2026.

These terms apply to every Hudson rental. They are written plainly on purpose. If anything here is unclear, ask us before you book.

1. Who can rent

Drivers must be 21 or over and hold a licence valid for at least one year. Drivers aged 21 to 24 pay a surcharge of $25 per day, added automatically to the quote. Every additional driver must be present at the counter with their licence.

2. What the price includes

The total shown at booking covers the daily rate, any options you chose, the young driver surcharge where it applies, and all taxes and airport fees. There is nothing further to pay at the counter unless you change the booking.

3. Mileage

Rentals include 150 miles per day. Beyond that the rate is $0.29 per mile, or add unlimited mileage for $15 per day at booking.

4. Fuel

Collect the vehicle with a full tank and return it full. We do not charge a refuelling markup if you bring it back at the level you took it.

5. Changes and cancellation

  • Cancel free up to 24 hours before pick-up.
  • Inside 24 hours, one day's rate is retained.
  • Change dates or vehicle free of charge, subject to availability.
  • No-shows are charged one day's rate.

6. Damage and insurance

Without the collision damage waiver you are liable for damage up to the value of the vehicle. With it, your liability is capped at $250. Neither covers damage caused by driving under the influence, off-road use, or an unlisted driver.

7. Your information

We collect the name, licence number, address and contact details needed to rent you a vehicle, and nothing else. We do not sell personal information.

8. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of New York.
